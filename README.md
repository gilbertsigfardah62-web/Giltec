# BiasharaFlow TZ

A prototype Tanzania-focused business intelligence app that helps an owner:
- Manage business flow (supplier + transport + charges).
- Estimate profit and probability of profit.
- Apply Lewin's theorem of behavior (`B = f(P, E)`).
- Add dopamine-style motivation nudges after progress milestones.
- Compare **world market price** and **Tanzania local price**.
- Predict price rise pressure from factors like oil shocks and war risk.
- Produce stock-buy signals (including examples like **CRDB**).

It includes guiding phrases:
- "Logic will get you from A to B. Imagination will take you everywhere."
- "I am rooted, but I flow."

## Run

```bash
python3 app.py
```

## What this app adds to the real world

- Gives small business owners a clear way to combine operations cost, market pricing, and simple forecasting in one flow.
- Encourages data-driven choices on suppliers, logistics, and margin planning.
- Surfaces early warning signs when global risk may push local prices up.

## What happens without it

- Decisions remain fragmented and mostly manual.
- Higher risk of underestimating transport/extra charges.
- Slower response to world-market shocks affecting Tanzania prices.

## Important note

Stock outputs are **educational prototype signals**, not financial advice. Connect to live market data feeds before real investment decisions.
